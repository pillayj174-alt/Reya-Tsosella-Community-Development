ABOUT REYA TSOSELLA

A community forum built around people and possibility.

Reya Tsosella Community Development and Employment Forum is a community-focused non-profit organisation associated with Ward 14 in Welkom, South Africa.

The organisation presents itself as a Jobs & Development Forum, with a focus on employment opportunities, community development and engagement.
